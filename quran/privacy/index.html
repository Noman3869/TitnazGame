Titanz Quran — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Titanz Quran ("the app") is a free Quran app made by Titanz Game ("we", "us"). It has no ads, no analytics SDKs and no tracking. This page explains the small amount of data the app uses and why.

Using the app without an account

You can use every feature without signing in. Your bookmarks, last read page, reading goal and settings are stored only on your device.

Location

  • If you allow location access, the app uses your approximate location to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction on your device. You can instead pick a city by hand.
  • If you turn on prayer notifications, the app sends a rounded location (about 1 km accuracy), your time zone and your prayer settings to our server so it can keep your prayer reminders up to date. We do not track your movements and do not share location with anyone.

Notifications

To send reminders and announcements, the app stores a push notification token for your device, the platform (iOS or Android) and the app version. The token is deleted when you turn notifications off or uninstall the app.

Optional account (Sign in with Apple or Google)

If you choose to sign in, we store:

  • your email address and name (as provided by Apple or Google; with Apple you may hide your email),
  • an account ID,
  • your bookmarks (including any notes you write), last read page and daily reading progress, and your app settings,

so they can sync between your devices. This data is used only to provide the sync feature.

Quran text, translations and audio

Translations and recitation audio are downloaded from our server and from the Quran Foundation / Quran.com content services. These requests contain normal technical information (such as your IP address) that those services need to deliver the content; we do not use it to identify you.

Where data is stored

Account and notification data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and push notifications are delivered through Expo, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • Delete your account at any time in the app from the Account screen (Delete account). This permanently removes your account and all synced data from our server.
  • Turning off notifications removes your device token and location from our server.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what is described above for optional sync.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
